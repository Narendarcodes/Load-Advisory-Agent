What the estimate covers

The calculator adds principal and interest only. Any charges that apply to your offer are shown in your application summary before you accept. This site does not collect payments.

What is never included in the estimate

  • Charges specific to your offer
  • Changes to the product rate after your offer
  • Late payment consequences

Read the terms of use and privacy notice.

Is the eligibility check an approval?

No. It is an indicative estimate based on what you tell us. It does not check your credit file and it does not promise a loan.

Do I need to share Aadhaar or PAN to check eligibility?

No. The indicative check uses income, employment and existing commitments only. Identity checks happen later, inside the application, and only if you continue.

Who sees my documents?

You and the review team. Files are private by default and downloads are re-checked against your account every time. The assistant never receives raw identity numbers.

What happens if my application is not approved?

You see the reasons that applied to your case, not a generic score. You can correct information if it was wrong, or start a new application if your circumstances change.

Can I talk to someone?

Yes. Ask the assistant for an explanation, or use feedback to ask for a person to review your case.

Ready to see where you stand?

The check takes a few minutes and does not affect your credit score.
