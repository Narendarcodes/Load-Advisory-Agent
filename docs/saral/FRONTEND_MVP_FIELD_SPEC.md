# Frontend MVP field spec (salaried only)

> **DRAFT, design only.** Generated by `python -m contracts.catalogue_v2.build` from `contracts/catalogue_v2/`. Nothing here is wired into ZEN, and nothing here is live. Bajaj Finance is a *public reference lender, not a partner*. ZEN is the authority for decisions; Bajaj's published numbers are indicative public guidance (user, 2026-11-03 23:07 IST).

Machine-readable: [`contracts/frontend_fields.json`](../../contracts/frontend_fields.json). Seven steps (user decision 2026-11-03 22:50 IST). Dropped: references, co-applicant, self-employed, education level, dependants, and the applicant-typed credit score (the score comes from the bureau only). Gender and marital status are KYC-only and never ZEN inputs. Father's/spouse name is optional KYC.

**Soft check (end of step 2).** Runs PL-001, PL-003, PL-008 (on the declared net income), PL-029, PL-069..PL-072, PL-076, PL-109, PL-110 and an indicative PL-125 offer. Because it pulls the bureau, the bureau consent is taken at step 2, separately, and shown again in step 7. The soft-check result is indicative and is re-run on verified data at submission.

Handling: DECLARED = typed by the applicant; EXTRACTED = read by OCR; VERIFIED = confirmed by a synthetic API or cross-check. The assessed value is the verified one where it exists (for income, the lower of payslip and bank, PL-009).
