# Reason disclosure policy

> **DRAFT, design only.** Generated by `python -m contracts.catalogue_v2.build` from `contracts/catalogue_v2/`. Nothing here is wired into ZEN, and nothing here is live. Bajaj Finance is a *public reference lender, not a partner*. ZEN is the authority for decisions; Bajaj's published numbers are indicative public guidance (user, 2026-11-03 23:07 IST).

## Principle

A borrower is told **which area** held the application back, in plain language, and what they can do. They are never told a threshold, a rule id, an exact value, the check logic, or that they were flagged. Underwriters see `internal_reason`, which may carry values and thresholds; it is role-restricted and never reaches the borrower or the borrower-facing agent.

Example: say "Your employment history did not meet our stability requirement for this loan." Never say "you have 4 months and we need 6".

## Regulatory and lender basis

- **RBI rejection-reason duty for NBFCs: NOT FOUND in the current NBFC directions (checked on rbi.org.in, 2026-11-03).** The Reserve Bank of India (Non-Banking Financial Companies – Responsible Business Conduct) Directions, 2025 (RBI/DOR/2025-26/362, November 28, 2025; checked both as issued and as updated on July 1, 2026) go from para 12 (acknowledgement of loan applications) to para 13 (sanction letter); no paragraph obliges an NBFC to give, in writing or otherwise, the reason for rejecting a loan application (https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_ViewMasDirections.aspx?id=12942). By contrast, the All India Financial Institutions RBC Directions, 2025, para 13, say: "An AIFI shall convey in writing, the main reason / reasons which, have led to rejection of the loan applications within the stipulated time for all categories of loans irrespective of any threshold limit" (https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_ViewMasDirections.aspx?id=12968). Whether the superseded Scale Based Regulation Directions, 2023 carried such a duty for NBFCs is UNVERIFIED (the PDF is behind a captcha). **Written rejection reasons stay as Saral policy (ADR-0010 #4), not an RBI requirement for NBFCs.** Saral gives the main reason area in writing in the decision notice, modelled on the AIFI/bank standard. See project-management/RBI_VERIFICATION.md.
- RBI-RBC-NBFC-2025#019: "All communications to the borrower shall be in the vernacular language or a language as understood by the borrower." Messages are written so they translate cleanly (the portal supports en, hi, te).
- BFL-FAQ-REJECTION#000 (Bajaj): "We will send you an SMS on your registered mobile number regarding your rejection."
- BFL-PL-OVERVIEW#018 (Bajaj) lists common rejection reasons at the level of areas (score, FOIR, probation, document mismatch, recent applications, settlements/write-offs), which is the level these categories keep.

## Building the borrower message for a decision

1. Take the rule results of the stored decision (not RAG).
2. DECLINE: list the distinct categories of the failing DECLINE rules (at most three, in group order), each with its message. Sensitive hits are not listed; if one is present the application goes to review instead of being declined on that basis.
3. MANUAL_REVIEW: one line that the application is with the team; add the generic DETAILS_NOT_VERIFIED line if any review rule is sensitive; add document requests for any MORE_INFORMATION_REQUIRED rules.
4. MORE_INFORMATION_REQUIRED: list what to provide, from `borrower_message_when_missing`.
5. COUNTER_OFFER: say a lower amount or longer tenure is available and show the offered amount, EMI and tenure (the offer is the borrower's own figure, not a threshold). Do not explain the cap that bound it.
6. Always add that the outcome is an eligibility assessment, not a sanction.
