The runtime's start-up notice — *"Listing and clearing threads need a caller identity… GET /threads and
POST /threads/clear return HTTP 403 until then"* — is CopilotKit's own warning about its thread-listing
endpoints. It is noted here rather than passed over: this portal does not use those endpoints (the
borrower's conversation list comes from `loan_advisory_portal.api.agent.list_conversations`, scoped by
Frappe ownership), and a 403 default is the safe direction. It is recorded as an open observation in the
readiness report.
